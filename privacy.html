Privacy Policy

Rclone-project is a personal OAuth application used by its owner to manage their own Google Drive accounts through rclone. It is not a service offered to the general public.

Information Accessed

Within the permissions granted by the user, the application accesses Google Drive files and associated metadata, such as file names, folder structures, file sizes, and modification times.

How Information Is Used

Access is used only to perform file management operations requested by the owner, including listing, uploading, downloading, organizing, and deleting files.

Storage and Security

OAuth tokens and configuration settings are stored in the rclone environment controlled by the owner. Downloaded files and cached data are stored in locations configured by the owner. The owner is responsible for securing these environments and storage locations.

Sharing and Disclosure

The application operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or send it to separate analytics services. Data is transferred between Google Drive and the storage locations selected by the owner as necessary to perform requested operations.

Retention and Deletion

Locally stored configuration, tokens, downloaded files, and cached data remain until removed by the owner or deleted according to the configured cache settings.

Revoking Access

The owner may revoke the application's access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously downloaded files, local caches, or configuration files. These can be deleted separately from the rclone environment.
